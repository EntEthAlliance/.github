The **Enterprise Ethereum Alliance (EEA)** is the member-led nonprofit connecting institutions to Ethereum through intelligence, practitioners, open standards and institutional peers.

This is our public technical workspace: specifications, working group publications, and research exploring security, privacy, interoperability and tokenization.

**[Start here](https://github.com/EntEthAlliance/start-here)** · **[EEA website](https://entethalliance.org/)** · **[Ethereum Intelligence](https://intelligence.entethalliance.org/)**

Published as an **individual research contribution by Redwan Meslem**, not an EEA standard or member consensus. ## Participate

- **Review a specification:** start with the [EthTrust public comment channel](https://github.com/EntEthAlliance/EthTrust-public) or [DLT interoperability feedback repository](https://github.com/EntEthAlliance/wg-dlt-interop). Follow the repository's submission rules and participation requirements.
- **Contribute to a project:** read its README, current status and contribution guidance, then check existing issues before proposing work.
- **Work with peers:** explore [EEA membership](https://entethalliance.org/membership/) and working group participation.

## Understand the status

**Published specifications**, **review drafts**, **research publications** and **experimental code** serve different purposes. Check each project's authoritative documents for its current version, approval and implementation status. EthTrust v3 is the approved baseline; the v4 review draft is not approved. Shibui is an experimental research model.

Earlier projects remain available in the **[archive](https://github.com/orgs/EntEthAlliance/repositories?type=archived)** for historical reference.
